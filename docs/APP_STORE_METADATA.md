## Description

Understand climate issues and turn what you learn into a practical next step.

The Climate Note publishes a carefully edited environmental article each week. Read the original article, then use clearly labeled AI additions to review the key problem, why it matters, and specific actions related to the story.

Features:

• Weekly climate and environmental articles
• Concise, plain-language AI summaries
• Article-specific action suggestions
• Private reflections and custom climate notes
• Weekly progress calendar and impact estimates
• Optional reminders
• Sign in with Apple or Google
• Read articles without creating an account
• Ad-free experience

AI never rewrites the original article. AI-generated material is labeled, and personal notes remain private to your account.
